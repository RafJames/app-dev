# Puss in Boots: The Last Wish ✨

> **"I am Death straight up."** — Death

This project is all about one of my favorite animated movies.

## 📺 About the Movie
* **Genre: ** Comedy, Family, Action
* **Creators: ** Joel Crawford
* **Setting: ** Del Mar, the Dark Forest, and the Kingdom of Far Far Away.

## 🌟 Main Characters
1. **Puss** - A fearless warrior.
2. **Kitty Softpaw** - Former partner of boots.
3. **Death** - The embodiment of death itself.
4. **Jack Horner** - A greedy baker.
5. **Goldilocks** - A girl who wanted a family.
6. **The three bears** - A family of bears consisting of Baby, Papa, Mama Bear
